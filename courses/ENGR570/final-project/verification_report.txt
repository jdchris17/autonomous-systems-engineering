4. KNOWN LIMITATIONS (not failures; things the system cannot do)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Constant acceleration is invisible.
     A steady horizontal acceleration is indistinguishable from a tilt.
     Every check passes and the fix is wrong by about 650 m per mm/s^2. The
     result stays VALID and carries the limitation in `assumptions`,
     `integrity.unverified_assumptions` and `accuracy_limitations`.
     Demonstrated by suite 4,
     test_constant_horizontal_acceleration_is_not_detected_and_not_denied.
  2. A wrong star match inside the centroid noise is invisible.
     A catalog identity error smaller than about 3.7 sigma of the centroid
     noise passes residual screening. Demonstrated by suite 4,
     test_a_mismatch_inside_the_noise_cannot_be_detected.
  3. A fixed calibration error is not observable at one attitude.
     The engine detects this, withholds the calibration estimate and reports
     the declared calibration uncertainty in full (suite 3). It cannot
     detect a calibration error LARGER than declared.
  4. Declared uncertainties are taken on trust.
     Coverage is honest only when the declared sigmas describe the real
     errors. Suite 5 shows coverage collapsing to a few percent when the
     same errors are present but not declared.
  5. A camera focal-length error has no declared-uncertainty parameter.
     It shows up in the star residuals and degrades the status, but it is
     not in the covariance.
  6. Refraction is a standard-atmosphere model.
     Temperature inversions and horizontal gradients are not modelled. Stars
     below 3 degrees altitude are not used.
  7. The independent reference path is not fully independent.
     Astropy assembles its frames differently but calls the same ERFA
     routines and reads the same bundled IERS table. Suite 2 detects errors
     in how the engine arranges the physics, not errors inside ERFA or in
     the Earth-orientation data.
  8. Earth-orientation prediction uncertainty uses a recalled formula.
     The growth law attributed to IERS Bulletin A (Module 8) has not been
     checked against the bulletin.
  9. No real data.
     Star detection, centroiding and catalog matching happen before this
     interface (ENGR 560). No real image, IMU record or site has been used.
     All error magnitudes are illustrative assumptions.
