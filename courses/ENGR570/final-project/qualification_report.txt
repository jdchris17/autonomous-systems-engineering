ENDURIA NAVIGATION ENGINE - PERFORMANCE QUALIFICATION (Work Package D)
========================================================================
Every number below is the output of solve_navigation_fix() on a simulated batch.
Error magnitudes are illustrative assumptions; Phase 1 (6-10 NM = 11.1-18.5 km) and
Phase 2 (100-200 m) are objectives, not device specifications.
Base seed 5710. Accuracy statistics are over trials that produced a fix; availability is separate.

1. REPRESENTATIVE SCENARIOS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A - Ideal reference: No noise or model error; random place and attitudes.
  B - Phase 1 baseline: Illustrative phone-class errors, stationary, one attitude, ten exposures.
  C - Calibrated stationary: Same hardware as B, twenty exposures at deliberately varied attitudes, calibration states estimated.
  D - Precision hardware: Reduced noise and bias, measured weather, deflection model to 1 arcsec, varied attitudes.
  E - Environmental stress: Precision hardware in a harsh, poorly known environment: -30 to +45 C, no weather data, pointing down to 65 deg zenith angle, thermal bias 5x, deflection model 3 arcsec, year-old Earth-orientation data.
